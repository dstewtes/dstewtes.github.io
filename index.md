## Bio

I am a post-baccalaureate research assistant at Reed College in Portland, Oregon.

## Research Interests

My research interests fall in the realm of **Privacy, Security, and Fairness in Statistics and Machine Learning**. Most of my research thus far has focused on **Differential Privacy**, but, in general, I am interested in **Cryptography**, with an emphasis on bridging the gap between theoretical guarantees and practical applications.
