## Bio

I am a post-baccalaureate research assistant at Reed College in Portland, Oregon. I graduated from Reed College with a degree in mathematics-computer science (as well as minors in dance and linguistics) in May 2026, and have worked as a research assistant since then. Outside the world of academia, I am a competitive Scottish Highland dancer (no, it is not the same as Riverdance) and have placed top five at the national championship four times (2023-2026) and placed top 20 at the world championship in 2023. I also dabble in other forms of dance such as Tap, Ballet, and Contemporary.


## Research Interests

My research interests fall in the realm of **Privacy, Security, and Fairness in Statistics and Machine Learning**. Most of my research thus far has focused on **Differential Privacy**, but, in general, I am interested in **Cryptography**, with an emphasis on bridging the gap between theoretical guarantees and practical applications.
